## Seasonal background images

The Settings thumbnails in app/src/assets/backgrounds/ are resized JPEG copies of
the photographs below. The full/ directory contains WebP copies (winter resized to
6000 pixels wide; the other full images keep their original pixel dimensions).
Metadata is removed from the full images. The app displays cover crops, blends
images with the window background and darkens/desaturates them in the dark look.
No endorsement is implied. These notices and image licenses cover both sizes.
